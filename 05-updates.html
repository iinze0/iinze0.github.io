Updates

Launcher updates and slot updates are separate.

  • Launcher: re-flash from its web page, or use its own update if it has one.
  • Slot apps: download the new .bin, copy to the card, re-install the slot from the launcher.
  • Card files (themes, scripts, .sub, .ir): just overwrite on the card and reboot.

See also troubleshooting.
