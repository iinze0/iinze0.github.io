Capabilities and limits

What the hardware can do, and what it cannot.

  • CC1101 covers the usual ISM bands. Transmit only where legal.
  • nRF24 is Plus only.
  • Official Flipper .fap apps do not run. They are ARM.
  • Rolling-code systems usually cannot be saved and replayed on stock firmware.
  • Range is short without a proper antenna.
