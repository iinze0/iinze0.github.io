What this covers

  • Launcher — bmorcelli or loznoc dual-boot. Flash once, then install apps into slots from the card.
  • Bruce — Wi-Fi, BLE, IR, Sub-GHz, BadUSB, JS scripts, App Store. Runs alone or in a slot.
  • Flipper-style port — Sor3nt ESP32 port. Needs the matching sdcard.zip unpacked on the card.
  • Remote — the launcher opens a Wi-Fi page. Your phone joins it and drives the screen and wheel.
