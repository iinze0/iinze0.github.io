Antennas

The CC1101 needs an antenna that matches the band you transmit on.

  • 433.92 MHz — common in EU and Australia
  • 868 MHz — EU
  • 315 / 915 MHz — US / North America

Internal whip or SMA. Match the whip to the band. See also regional frequencies.
