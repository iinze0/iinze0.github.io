SD card

FAT32. 8–32 GB is the painless size. Insert it before the first launcher boot if you want slots.

  • Format on a computer, not on the device.
  • The launcher writes slot metadata and settings to the card.
  • Apps (Bruce, Flipper port, etc.) store their own files on the same card.
  • USB disk mode, when available, shows the card as a drive.
