Bands

Frequencies by region.
Set the band before you transmit.

The CC1101 covers roughly 300–348, 387–464, and 779–928 MHz. Transmit only where legal. The antenna must match the band.

RegionPrimary bandsNotes
Europe / UK / Norway / EEA433.92 MHz, 868 MHzDuty cycle limits apply on 868. 915 is not the EU plan.
USA / Canada315 MHz, 433.92 MHz, 915 MHz915 is the usual Sub-GHz choice.
Australia / New Zealand433.92 MHz915 plan exists; confirm local rules.

Antenna matching

  • One whip = one band.
  • SMA male into the shell’s SMA female. RP-SMA does not connect.
  • External antenna (K268-01) lets you swap whips. Internal is pocket-friendly but harder to change.
