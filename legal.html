Setup Install Remote Guide Legal

Worldwide

Own hardware only.
The same acts are illegal almost everywhere.

This is a hardware and install guide. It is not a pentest playbook. A disclaimer does not make an act legal.

What is fine

  • Flashing your own board.
  • Reading and writing tags you bought.
  • Capturing remotes you own, then replaying them at your own gate or door.
  • Wi-Fi and BLE tests on an access point you control, isolated from neighbours.
  • IR against your own TV or devices.
  • nRF24 between modules you own.

If you cannot point at the owner and the permission, do not press transmit.

What is not fine

Using these tools on someone else’s property, network, badge, car, gate, or device without clear permission is unauthorised access, interception, or computer misuse in almost every country. Common examples:

  • United States — Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA). Unauthorised access or exceeding authorised access to a computer system. Fines and prison. A Wi-Fi network, badge reader, car immobiliser, or gate controller counts.
  • United Kingdom — Computer Misuse Act 1990. Unauthorised access, unauthorised access with intent, and unauthorised acts with intent to impair. Prison possible.
  • European Union / EEA — National laws implementing the EU Cybercrime Directive and Budapest Convention. Unauthorised access, interception, and data interference are criminal offences. Penalties vary by country.
  • Canada — Criminal Code sections on unauthorised use of a computer and mischief in relation to data. Fines and prison.
  • Australia — Criminal Code Act 1995 (Division 477). Unauthorised access, modification, or impairment of data. Serious penalties.
  • Norway — Penal Code (straffeloven) chapter 21, sections 201–205. Unauthorised access, interference, and related offences. Courts have given prison for access alone (example: HR-2026-1908-A, 120 days).
  • Most other countries — Equivalent unauthorised-access, interception, and computer-misuse laws. Customs can also seize the device.

Aggravating factors that often raise the penalty: more than one victim, a repeated pattern, anything saved to the SD card, selling a clone, doing it at a workplace, or doing it near a vehicle. The SD card and flash are evidence. Deleting a file does not erase the wear-levelled copy.

Radio rules

Transmitting outside the legal ISM plan for your country, over power, or as harmful interference is an administrative offence (and sometimes a crime) even if no door opens. Duty-cycle limits apply on many bands. 915 MHz is not an EU plan. Set the band correctly before any transmit. See frequencies.

What this guide will not contain

No capture/replay steps for systems you do not own, no rolling-code bypass, no gate or car-fob opening of someone else’s property, no NFC clone of an access card you were not issued, no deauth, evil twin, portal or handshake guide, no jammer settings, no BadUSB payload that types itself into a machine you do not own. Those are methods for unauthorised access. Official firmware projects document their own menus. Use those on your own lab.
